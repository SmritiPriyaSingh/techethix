# Mastering CEH v13: Practice Strategy & Module Breakdown

As a Certified Ethical Hacker (CEHv13 by EC-Council), building structured module-by-module practice questions was a key part of my exam preparation strategy. This inspired the creation of the **CEH Portal web platform**.

---

## Core Focus Modules in CEH v13

1. **Footprinting & Reconnaissance**: Using Shodan, Nmap, Maltego, and Google Dorking for passive & active intelligence gathering.
2. **System Hacking & Vulnerability Analysis**: Password cracking (John the Ripper, Hydra), privilege escalation techniques, and malware analysis.
3. **Web Application Hacking**: OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities (SQL Injection, XSS, CSRF) tested via Burp Suite and OWASP ZAP.
4. **Wireless & IoT Security**: Aircrack-ng suite, WPA2 enterprise cracking, and Zigbee/CCTV security.
